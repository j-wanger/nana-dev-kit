## Summary

Planned AND implemented Phase 99 in one session. The maintainer reframed the direction at the gate: pull
**rung-C** (contract-driven delegation to downstream/local workers) forward from "future / plumbing" and
treat it as the kit's own thesis applied one level up — a frozen **contract** (outcome objectives +
deterministic guardrails, the hooks pattern) governs a downstream worker so it stays truthful, **plus an
HTML dashboard** as the human steering surface, because in-session text streams faster than the maintainer
can process and steer. Three pillars — (1) contract + fidelity spine, (2) downstream-worker execution,
(3) the dashboard — and **pillar 3 is built first** (maintainer's "dashboard-first" choice).

Phase 99 ships a **render-only direction dashboard**: a 4th static-HTML generator (`generate-direction.py`
→ `docs/direction.html`, `make direction`) that renders the `/dev-plan` direction gate (recommendation,
option cards with the recommended one badged, cost-sorted assumptions + positions, orienting context) from
a structured **direction brief** (`.dev-wiki/direction-brief.json`). The maintainer reviews visually, then
answers the gate in-session. A minimal dev-plan integration (`direction-brief.md` companion, wired into the
assumption-gate step) makes every future gate emit a brief and render before asking for positions.

## Decisions

- [[direction-dashboard]] (high) — render-only direction dashboard, dashboard-first, pillar 3 of the rung-C
  program. A1 render-only **accept** (bottleneck is processing dense text, not decide-latency); A2
  generator-vs-live-server **don't-know → down-scoped** to the one-shot generator (a strict subset of a
  future watching server — costs nothing toward it; live-server filed to Blockers); A3 first-surface =
  direction gate **accept**; A4 small dev-plan brief-emission **accept**. Direction gate all_accept:false.

## Method notes

- **Prior art de-risked it.** Three static-HTML generators already exist (`generate-report.py`,
  `generate-workflow.py`, `generate-delivery-report.py`) sharing a house style and writing to `docs/` —
  none on the direction/steering side. The dashboard is the 4th, self-contained, matching the pattern (no
  shared-helper refactor of the other three — surgical-changes discipline).
- **Controls-first / [[HEU-012]].** The test asserts the rendered HTML CONTAINS the brief content (not
  file-existence), the recommended option is badged, AND a seeded malformed brief makes the generator fail
  loud (non-zero + stderr). RED was genuine: the missing-required-field control failed against the lenient
  T1 generator (rc=0) before T2 added validation.
- **Dogfood.** The first thing the dashboard renders is Phase 99's own gate — exercising the realistic
  recommended≠chosen case (recommended "Thin end-to-end slice", chosen "Dashboard-first").
