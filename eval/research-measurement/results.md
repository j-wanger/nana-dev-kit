# Phase 58 — Residual Research Delta Measurement

> Date: 2026-05-28. Measures the **residual** quality delta from Step 2.7 active domain
> research, vs the Phase-55 baseline (less-prescriptive specs, no research). NOT a re-claim
> of the headline +1.75 — part of that was already captured by Phase 55.

## Method

One held-out phase objective the wiki does **not** cover: *"Add schema-validated structured
outputs for subagent returns."* Two approaches generated by independent clean-context
subagents, scored blind by a third (calibrated: 5 reserved for excellent, most work 3–4):

- **Baseline (A):** objective only. No research. (= Phase-55 behavior.)
- **Research-injected (B):** objective + 2 distilled findings from a bounded web pass
  ([F1] arXiv 2408.02442 "Let Me Speak Freely?"; [F2] dottxt "Say What You Mean").

Same objective, same judge, two dimensions (decision_quality, reasoning_quality), 1–5.

## Coverage gate (both branches verified against the real wiki)

- **COVERED topic** (heuristic injection / context-dilution budget) → wiki-query hits
  `wiki/articles/patterns/context-injection-budget.md` → research **SKIPPED**, zero external calls. ✓
- **UNCOVERED topic** (structured outputs / JSON-schema / constrained decoding) → wiki-query
  returns nothing → research **FIRED** (3 searches, 1 fetch, within the ≤10 tool-call budget). ✓

The gate behaves as designed: the common (covered) case is free; research fires only on the gap.

## Result

| Dimension | Baseline (A) | Research (B) | Δ |
|-----------|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| decision_quality | 4 | 4 | 0 |
| reasoning_quality | 3 | 4 | **+1** |
| **composite** | **3.5** | **4.0** | **+0.5** |

Judge verdict: **B better**. Non-theater confirmed — research produced a *decision-changing*
finding the baseline missed: **"reasoning-field-first schema ordering"** (emit a reasoning
field before answer fields so autoregressive decoding preserves chain-of-thought under a
structured-output constraint), grounded in [F1]+[F2]. B cited the findings at the specific
decision they informed, satisfying the anti-theater guard.

Not strictly dominant: **A** named a decision **B** missed (versioned schemas for independent
orchestrator/subagent evolution). So research lifted *reasoning* quality but did not uniformly
beat the baseline on every decision.

## Honest caveats (the delta is suggestive, not proven)

- **n = 1.** Single topic, single judge, no repeats. Within-sample only; no variance estimate.
  The +0.5 composite is below the project's own "Δ ≥ 0.5 meaningful, variance < 0.5" reasoning-eval
  bar — it sits *at* the threshold with unknown variance. Treat as directional.
- **Topic favors research.** "Structured outputs" is a live area with strong recent literature;
  research helps most exactly here. A topic with thin/SEO-only web coverage would likely show ~0.
- **Provenance bit:** [F1] was fetched (arXiv abstract). **[F2] came from a search snippet** —
  the dottxt fetch hit a `.co→.ai` redirect that wasn't followed. Per the Step 2.7 spec,
  [F2] is usable for the approach but would **not** be persisted as a sourced wiki fact
  (snippet ≠ source). The guard fired correctly in a real run.

## Reading

Active research showed a **small, real, non-theatrical** residual lift (+1 reasoning, +0.5
composite) on an uncovered topic, driven by a genuinely decision-changing finding — *and* the
gate kept covered topics free. That is the mechanism working as intended. But n=1 at the
significance threshold is not proof the feature pays for its complexity across the board; the
honest claim is "promising on research-rich gaps, likely ~0 on research-poor ones."

→ **Checkpoint 2 decision (keep / trim / cut) is the user's**, framed from this evidence.
