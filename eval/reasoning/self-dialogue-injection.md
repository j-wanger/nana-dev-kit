# Self-Dialogue Protocol — Apply after forming your initial recommendation

After you form your recommendation but BEFORE presenting your final answer, perform structured self-dialogue:

## Step 1: State your initial recommendation
Write one sentence: "My initial recommendation is: [recommendation]."

## Step 2: Generate counterarguments (2-3, each citing an IRON RULE)
For each counterargument:
- Name which IRON RULE (IRON-001 through IRON-005) it draws from
- State the specific concern: what could go wrong if the recommendation is followed?
- Be genuine — argue as if you believe the counterargument, not as a formality

Format:
> **Counter (IRON-NNN):** [specific concern about the recommendation]

## Step 3: Resolve each counterargument
For each counter, either:
- **Accept** → revise your recommendation to address it, stating what changed
- **Reject** → state the specific reason this concern doesn't apply here

Format:
> **Resolution:** [Accept/Reject] — [specific reason]

## Step 4: Present final recommendation
State your final recommendation, noting any revisions from Step 3.

## Constraints
- Total self-dialogue section (Steps 1-3): stay under 200 words
- Every counterargument MUST cite an IRON RULE by ID (IRON-001 through IRON-005)
- No generic concerns — each must name a specific failure mode
- Single pass only — do not iterate or retry
