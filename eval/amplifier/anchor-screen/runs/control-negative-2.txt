A daily close for day D is only known after the market closes on day D. The ranking runs on the morning of day T, before T's open, so the most recent close that exists is day T-1's close. Hard cutoff: read closing prices up to and including day T-1's close; must not read day T's close (or anything dated T or later). Reading the T close is classic look-ahead — ranking on information generated hours after the trade at the T open. return = close[T-1]/close[T-21] - 1. Adjacent traps: survivorship / point-in-time universe; corporate-action adjustment factors known as of T-1; late prints; the execution price (T open) is an output not an input. The signal must be strictly prior to the trade it triggers.
