On the morning of day T before the open: day T's close does not exist yet; day T-1's close is the most recent close that exists. The most recent close the function may read is the close of day T-1. The 20-day trailing return window is the closes on trading days T-20 through T-1 inclusive: return = close[T-1]/close[T-21] - 1. May read up to and including day T-1's close; must NOT read day T's close and any close from day T onward. Justification is look-ahead bias / information availability — on the morning of T, T's close is a future price. Execution at T's open is fine (contemporaneous with the decision); close[T] is the forbidden future price. Watch point-in-time data (late corrections, retroactive split/dividend adjustments) and survivorship / as-of universe.
