The EU Anti-Money-Laundering Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2024/1624), adopted in the 2024 AML package, sets an EU-wide upper limit of 10,000 euros on cash payments in the course of trade/business (persons trading in goods or providing services; a licensed car dealership is in scope). Member States may set lower national limits but 10,000 euros is the harmonized EU-wide ceiling; it applies to a single transaction or linked operations, and the provisions apply from 10 July 2027. Determination: a 12,000-euro cash payment exceeds the 10,000-euro limit and is NOT permitted as a single cash transaction. The buyer must settle the portion above 10,000 euros (or the whole amount) by a traceable non-cash method. Germany historically had no national cash cap, so this EU-wide 10,000 euro ceiling is the binding constraint.
