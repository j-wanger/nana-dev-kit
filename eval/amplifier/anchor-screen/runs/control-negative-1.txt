Read: the 20 daily closes ending at day T-1 (closes for days T-20 through T-1 inclusive). Must not read: the close of day T itself, and any price from day T onward (open, intraday, or close). The ranking runs pre-open on day T, so the information set is frozen at that instant; day T's close will not exist until ~6.5 hours after the screener has already bought at the open. Reaching for "the latest 20 closes" naively includes the T close — that is look-ahead bias, producing fictional backtest returns. The cutoff falls between the close of T-1 and the close of T. Use an explicit as-of filter (date <= T-1), trading days not calendar days, and confirm T-1 is the final settled close. Even day T's open is kept out of the ranking signal (execution price, not signal input).
