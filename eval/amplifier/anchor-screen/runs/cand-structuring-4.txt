This is a textbook structuring pattern under 31 U.S.C. 5324. Nine cash deposits all in the $8,600-$9,400 band, deliberately just under the $10,000 CTR threshold, split across three branches over four days, total ~$80,000, no explanation. The sub-$10,000 amounts are not a safe harbor — the clustering and branch-splitting are the red flag. Aggregation is at the institution level across branches. The bank must file a SAR for suspected structuring (suspicious activity), file CTRs for any day the aggregated cash exceeds $10,000, and must NOT tip off the customer. Structuring to evade reporting is itself a crime regardless of whether the funds are clean. Escalate to BSA/AML compliance for EDD and enhanced monitoring.
