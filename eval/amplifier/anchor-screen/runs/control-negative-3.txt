A daily closing price for a given day only exists after that day's market close. On the morning of day T before the open, day T's close does not exist yet — reading close[T] is the classic look-ahead bug. Day T-1's close is the most recent price that exists. The cutoff falls between the close of T-1 (allowed, latest allowed) and the close of T (forbidden). return_20d = close[T-1] / close[T-21] - 1. Read the 20 daily closes from close[T-21] through close[T-1] inclusive; these are trading days, calendar gaps skipped. Must not read close[T] or any later close, and must not touch open[T] (execution price, not signal). Governing rule: point-in-time / no look-ahead — a signal may only use information observable at the decision instant (pre-open on day T).
