The cutoff falls at day T-1's close. The function may read closing prices through and including day T-1's close, and must not read day T's close (nor any later price). At the instant you rank (pre-market day T), the most recent fully-formed daily close is day T-1's. return = close[T-1] / close[T-21] - 1. Reading close[T] is the classic look-ahead bias: ranking what you buy at T's open using a price knowable only hours after the trade — it manufactures returns no real execution can capture. Subtle trap: off-by-one from a naive slice that grabs a row dated T; key off the date (date <= T-1), assert max(date) < T. Caveat: vendor data revisions / late-arriving adjusted closes and stale/halted names — use point-in-time data. Never read close dated >= T.
